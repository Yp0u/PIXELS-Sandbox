> **PIXELS Sandbox** is a high-performance procedural cellular automata and physics sandbox engine. Imagine a world where every pixel isn't just a color, but possesses mass, temperature, density, electrical conductivity, and radiation levels. 
> Build complex electrical circuits, create controlled (or completely chaotic) nuclear reactions, grow forests, and watch them be consumed by acid mist. Everything runs in real-time with a deeply interconnected elemental system.

## 🔥 Key Features

*   **Deep Chemistry & Physics:** Over 60 unique elements (ranging from water, sand, and lava to uranium, thermoelectric generators, lasers, and hazardous gases). Materials melt, evaporate, burn, conduct electricity, and decay in a vacuum.
*   **Dynamic Fields:** The simulation goes far beyond simple pixel collisions. The engine calculates thermodynamics (heat transfer), pressure differentials, energy accumulation in batteries, and the spread of background radiation.
*   **Procedural Audio:** Absolutely no pre-recorded `.wav` or `.mp3` files. All sounds (explosions, acid hissing, electrical sparks) are mathematically generated on the fly using ADSR envelopes and dynamic stereo panning relative to the camera's position.  
*   **Hardcore Optimization:** Written in Python, but runs with the speed of compiled languages. Thanks to JIT compilation (Numba) and matrix math (NumPy), the engine can process hundreds of thousands of active particles without dropping frames.  
*   **Analytical Layers:** Switch between view modes to see your world through a thermal camera, pressure sensor, Geiger counter, or energy scanner.  
*   **Advanced Tools:** Select and copy entire mechanical builds, draw precise lines, and adjust your brush interaction radius.

## 🚀 Quick Start

All of this chaos and underlying physics is packed into a single, standalone `.exe` file. No environment setup, no Python installation, and no hunting for dependencies—the engine is ready to launch the moment you download it. 

Ready to see what happens when you drop C4 into a highly pressurized tank of lava and uranium? Grab the executable in the **[Releases](https://github.com/Yp0u/PIXELS-Sandbox/releases)** section and start experimenting.
